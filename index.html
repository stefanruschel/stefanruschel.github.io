Research Interests

I study delay-based dynamics and computing: how networks compute and learn with the time their signals take to travel. In the brain, white matter adapts conduction delays with learning; in photonic hardware, a delay is simply a length of path. I treat delays as adaptive variables that shape phase-locked states and travelling waves, building on the theory of dynamical systems with time delay, from excitable lasers to neural networks.
